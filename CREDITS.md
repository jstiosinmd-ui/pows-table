## Reuse status

These publicly accessible photographs were downloaded at the user's request for this personal catalog. Redistribution permission is not established. Credits are provenance, not a license. Review permission or replace photographs before public reuse where required.

## Instagram review

Sixteen official establishment profiles were checked. Public profile descriptions helped confirm hours and contact details, but the full photo grids were unavailable. An accessible Raion post image was reviewed; its exterior sign photograph was not selected over the clearer interior photograph. WRUN and Go-To Matcha did not have a confirmed official Instagram profile in the research.
